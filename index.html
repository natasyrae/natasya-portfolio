How to play

  • WASD
    Walk around

    Focus the map and use WASD or the arrow keys to move. You can also click or tap a point to walk there.

  • E
    Explore a city

    Press E near a city marker to open its journal. Selecting a marker walks you there and opens it for you.

  • Esc
    Return to the map

    Close the journal to keep exploring from the same spot.
